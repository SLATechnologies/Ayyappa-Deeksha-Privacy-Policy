# AYYAPPA DEEKSHA Android Application Privacy Policy

**Developer:** SLA Technologies  
**Contact:** slatechnologies.apps@gmail.com  
**Last updated:** 11 November 2026

## 1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SLA Technologies handles information when you use the AYYAPPA DEEKSHA mobile application (the “App”).

The App is a free devotional application intended for a general audience. It is designed to work offline, with devotional content included in the App.

## 2. Information you may enter and save

The App does not require you to create an account, register, or sign in.

If you choose to use the App’s personal devotional features, you may enter information such as your name, Gothram, Pooja history, Mala Dharana or Deeksha start date, and information used to display your Deeksha day count and progress.

This information is stored locally in the App on your device. Based on the current implementation details provided by the developer, this information is not sent to SLA Technologies or uploaded to a developer-operated server.

## 3. How information is used

Information saved in the App is used only to provide on-device features, such as displaying your saved Pooja history and calculating or showing your Deeksha day count and progress.

SLA Technologies does not use this locally saved information for advertising, profiling, analytics, or marketing.

## 4. Data sharing and third-party services

The current version of the App is represented by the developer as not using third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, authentication, or cloud-storage SDKs.

SLA Technologies does not receive or disclose the devotional information you save in the App to third parties through the App.

The App is designed to work offline and does not require an internet connection for its described devotional features. This section must be reviewed if online content, external services, SDKs, or network features are added in a future version.

## 5. Storage, security and device backups

Your saved devotional information is stored on your device rather than in an account or cloud database operated by SLA Technologies.

Please protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep your operating system up to date. Anyone who can access your unlocked device or its app data may be able to see information saved in the App.

Depending on your Android version, device manufacturer, and backup settings, Android or a device backup service may back up app data. Such system-level backup behavior is controlled by your device and Android settings, not by SLA Technologies. The developer should verify the Android backup configuration before claiming that app data never leaves the device.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Your saved Pooja history remains on your device until you delete it using the App’s Clear History feature, or until the App’s data is cleared or the App is uninstalled, subject to any device-level backup or restore settings.

Use the App’s Clear History option to delete the saved Pooja history covered by that option. This may not necessarily remove other saved items, such as devotee details or Deeksha progress, unless the App’s implementation expressly includes them in the same deletion action.

To remove other locally stored App data, use Android device settings to clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. These actions may remove all locally stored App information. The exact result can depend on Android backup and restore settings.

The App does not provide user accounts, so there is no account to deactivate or delete and no account-deletion request process.

## 7. Children and general audience

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed to target children. The App does not require account registration. Users should only enter information they choose to store on their own device.

## 8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

SLA Technologies may update this Privacy Policy if the App’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the Privacy Policy URL, with a revised “Last updated” date. If the App begins collecting, transmitting, or sharing information, this policy and the App’s Google Play disclosures will be updated as applicable.

## 9. Contact us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the App’s privacy practices, contact SLA Technologies at slatechnologies.apps@gmail.com.
